	\begin{abstract}

		\noindent Personal notes on Theory of Computation, following
		Kozen's \emph{Automata and Computability}. Chapter~1 covers
		finite automata and regular languages, Chapter~2 covers
		context-free grammars and pushdown automata, Chapter~3 covers
		Turing machines and effective computability, and Chapter~4
		collects selected solutions to the miscellaneous exercises.

		\medskip

		using my own template. The template is available at:
		\url{https://github.com/karsayan2357/my-templates/tree/main/notes/notes}.

	\end{abstract}
